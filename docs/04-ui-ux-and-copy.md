# 🎨 UI/UX Design & Copywriting

## 1. Global Layout
*   **Header:** 
    *   Right side: Logo (Circle with 'N', brand color). Next to it: "وفاء للجمال" and below it "Nama Beauty".
    *   Middle: Navigation Menu (Home, Collection, About, Contact).
    *   Left side: Cart Icon with item count badge.
*   **Footer:** Links to Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund Policy, Contact Us. Copyright "2026 وفاء للجمال".
*   **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first design, highly optimized for iPhone screens (Snapchat/TikTok traffic).

## 2. Page Structures
*   **Alternating Sections:** On desktop, use alternating layouts for readability. E.g., Section 1: Image Left, Text Right. Section 2: Text Left, Image Right. On mobile, stack them (Image top, Text bottom).
*   **Product Pages:**
    *   Hero: Product Image (placeholder for now), Title, Subtitle, Star Reviews (e.g., 4.9/5 from 1,200 reviews).
    *   Pricing Tiers (199, 279, 349 SAR) as selectable cards.
    *   Sticky CTA at the bottom on mobile.
    *   Sections: The Problem (Pain), The Science (Ingredients), The Relief (Emotion/Result).
    *   Bottom: "Frequently Bought Together" (Cross-sells).

## 3. Trust & Authority Elements
*   **Badges:** Use icons for "الدفع عند الاستلام" (Cash on Delivery), "شحن سريع" (Fast Shipping), "مكونات معتمدة" (Approved Ingredients), "آمن على البشرة" (Skin Safe).
*   **Social Proof:** Add realistic-looking review sections with KSA names (e.g., "سارة العتيبي", "نورة م.").

## 4. Copywriting Tone (KSA Dialect & Clinical)
*   Blend professional clinical Arabic with welcoming KSA phrasing.
*   *Example CTA:* "اطلبي الآن والدفع عند الاستلام" (Order now, pay on delivery).
*   *Example Urgency:* "الكمية محدودة جداً بسبب الطلب العالي" (Stock is very limited due to high demand).
